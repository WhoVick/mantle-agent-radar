Most useful Mantle alpha does not start as a clean dashboard.
It shows up as DoraHacks rules, Telegram replies, judging criteria, and on-chain references.

Mantle Agent Radar collects those fragments into one source inbox and runs an agent scan.
The scan ranks each item by source quality, Mantle relevance, urgency, novelty, and investment utility.

Here the agent filters for deadline and deployment edge.
One signal says the prize map creates several money paths, not just one track prize.
The detail panel explains what happened, why it matters, the evidence behind it, and the recommended action.

The right panel shows the transformation from raw source text into a scored signal.
That is the core product: messy ecosystem context becomes investor-readable intelligence.

For high-confidence output, Radar generates a signal hash and commits it through SignalRegistry on Mantle mainnet.
Judges can verify the contract, the transaction, and the exact signal hash later.

The live demo, GitHub repo, DoraHacks BUIDL, and Mantle proof are all public.
Mantle Agent Radar is a working AI Alpha and Data MVP for tracking opportunities before they become obvious.
